# Luca Galati — personal academic website ({{ site.BaseURL }})
Luca Galati is a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at the Institute for Financial Innovation & Technology, LMU Munich School of Management, Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (Germany), and an Affiliated Researcher at the Digital Finance Cooperative Research Centre (Australia).
He studies how technology changes the design and efficiency of financial markets.
Research areas: market design and microstructure; FX benchmarks and execution; crypto and decentralised markets; networks and price discovery; accounting, governance and ESG.
Education: Ph.D. in Finance, University of Wollongong (2024); Doctorate in Accounting, University of Molise (2024).
