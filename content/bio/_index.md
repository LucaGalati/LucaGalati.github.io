title: "Bio & CV"
---

I am a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the LMU Institute for Financial Innovation and Technology (Germany) and an Affiliated Researcher at the Digital Finance Cooperative Research Centre (Australia). Previously, I completed a Post-Doctoral Research Fellowship in Accounting at the University of Bologna (Italy). Before my post-doctoral studies, I earned a Ph.D. in Finance from the University of Wollongong (Australia) and a Doctorate in Accounting from the University of Molise (Italy). I also served as an Industrial PhD Research Fellow in Market Microstructure at the Rozetta Institute (Australia), a Foreign Exchange Benchmark Surveillance Specialist at LSEG Data & Analytics (UK), and as Co-Founder of 110 Laude s.r.l. (Italy), a Fintech start-up.

I study how technology changes the design and efficiency of financial markets. My research combines financial economics, data science, and digital infrastructure to understand how trading protocols, exchanges, and decentralised systems should be designed. My publications appear in academic journals such as the Journal of Banking & Finance, Quantitative Finance, and The Journal of Futures Markets.
