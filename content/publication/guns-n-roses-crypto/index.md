abstract: "This paper examines how the July 13, 2024, assassination attempt on Donald Trump propagated through cryptocurrency markets. Using minute-by-minute data for the ten largest crypto-assets, we employ a BEKK-MGARCH framework to assess volatility dynamics and an event-study of cumulative abnormal returns (CARs) to identify the transmission mechanism. We document a pronounced intensification of volatility spillovers following the shock, indicating financial contagion and heightened cross-asset risk transmission.
