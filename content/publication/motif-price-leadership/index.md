abstract: "The influence one price exerts on related prices travels through the market before it dies out or returns to its source. We propose a measure of price leadership that exploits this property by following each price’s influence along a multivariate Markov chain network. Weighted random walks of at most three steps sort that influence into mass that exits at the first step, mass that travels outward, and mass that returns. The Motif Price Leadership Share assigns leadership on the outward mass. Unlike bilateral measures such as the Price Leadership Share, this framework separates influence that propagates from influence that stops at the first link or returns. An application to 28 Dow Jones constituents over 2005–2024 shows that the two shares agree on the four leading firms and move more than half of the others by five positions or more. The share of influence that stops at the first link rises significantly during the 2008–2009 financial crisis and the COVID-19 crash."
