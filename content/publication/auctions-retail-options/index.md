abstract: "Payment for order flow and its auctions rest on market fragmentation. We separate the auction from this structure using two markets on one index. Options on the S&P 500 ETF (SPY) are multi-listed and intermediated through payment for order flow, while options on the S&P 500 index (SPX) trade only on Cboe, without fragmentation or order-flow payments. When Cboe introduced auctions for small SPX orders in February 2021, effective spreads fell by four to six cents and price improvement rose, while market-maker revenue turned negative. SPY did not change. Auctions raise execution quality through competition, but only where the spread exceeds one tick."
