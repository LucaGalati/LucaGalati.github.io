To Fix or not to Fix, the Fix: Reassessing the Effectiveness of the 4 pm Fix

Matteo Benenchia, Luca Galati and Andrew Lepone · Pacific-Basin Finance Journal 93, 2025 · a pre-registered study

Every weekday at 4 pm in London, a few minutes of currency trading become the exchange rates that funds and index providers around the world use for the day. After a manipulation scandal the window was stretched from one minute to five; some say it should be twenty. This explorable follows nine years of trading data on six currency pairs to see what a longer window would and would not buy. Three short decks on the main path, three side paths, two bets, about fifteen minutes.
