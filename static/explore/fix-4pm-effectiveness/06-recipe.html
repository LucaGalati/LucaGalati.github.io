Change the average, not the window?

A longer window fights a rogue price by drowning it in more prices. Another way is to change the average itself. The authors point to an earlier finding that the median is less efficient than a trimmed or a winsorized mean (Evans et al., 2018), and suggest trying estimators such as Hodges–Lehmann.

Trimming drops the highest and lowest tenth of the prices; winsorizing pulls them in to the nearest remaining price; Hodges–Lehmann takes the median of the averages of every pair of prices. Move the slider to push the bad prints further out and watch each average.
