The plumbing of crypto trading

On a crypto exchange, getting out of bitcoin into real dollars can be costly, awkward and slow. A stablecoin lets a trader step out of bitcoin’s swings and hold a dollar without leaving the exchange, and it moves from one exchange to another quickly. Bitcoin investors may use one as a safe haven, and at times stablecoins have even traded at a premium to the dollar for exactly these conveniences.

So dollar coins became the plumbing of the market. Three of the five most traded cryptocurrencies are stablecoins, and on a single June day Tether changed hands for nearly twice as much as bitcoin.
