Intelligent Design: Stablecoins (In)stability and Collateral During Market Turbulence

Riccardo De Blasis, Luca Galati, Alexander Webb and Robert I. Webb · Financial Innovation, 2023

A stablecoin is a crypto token built to be worth exactly one dollar. In May 2022 one of the largest, TerraUSD, fell to a few cents, and the others moved too: some below a dollar, some above it. This short explorable shows how five coins hold up the same promise in five different ways, lets you stress each design, and then follows the paper through the week of the collapse. Two decks on the main path, three side paths, a few things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
