What this data can and cannot see

  • One eventA before-and-after around one minute, with no untouched coin to compare against. Anything else that happened in the second week counts as “after”: $MELANIA the next day, and the inauguration on 20 January, whose anticipation the paper itself links to the gains of the week before.
  • One exchangeGemini, a US exchange, and ten of the largest coins it lists, not the whole market. Spillovers could plausibly show there, the authors argue, because $TRUMP also trades on centralized exchanges (footnote 4).
