From Zero to Hero: Memecoins’ Spillover Effects in Cryptocurrency Markets

Luca Galati and Salvatore Perdichizzi · Economics Letters 253 (2025), 112381

In January 2025 the newly elected US president launched a memecoin of his own, and within a day it had overtaken Dogecoin, the largest memecoin until then. This short explorable follows what that did to ten coins that were already trading, minute by minute: whether they swung harder, whether they began to move in step with Bitcoin, and which of them gained or lost. Two decks on the main path, two side paths, two bets and a few things to play with; about ten minutes.
