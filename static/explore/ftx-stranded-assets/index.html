An explorable explanation

Market Behaviors Around Bankruptcy and Frozen Funds Withdrawal:Trading Stranded Assets on FTX

Luca Galati, Alexander Webb and Robert I. Webb · Journal of Economics and Business 133, 2025

For almost four days in November 2022, customers of FTX, then one of the largest crypto exchanges, could still trade the coins in their accounts but could not take them out. This explorable follows what they did with those stranded balances, what trading cost them, and how far prices inside the exchange drifted from the rest of the market. Two decks on the main path, three side paths, two bets and a few things to play with: about fifteen minutes.
