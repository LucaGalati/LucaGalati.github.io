Luca Galati and Carmine Russo · Finance Research Letters 103, 2026

On a Saturday evening in July 2024, a shot was fired at Donald Trump at a rally in Pennsylvania, and crypto markets, which never close, could answer at once. Follow ten of the largest tokens through the week before and the week after: which way they moved, whether they moved together, and how much a single event can show.
