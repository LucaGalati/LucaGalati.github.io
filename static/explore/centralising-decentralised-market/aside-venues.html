Side path Theory

Why have many venues at all?

The toy shows only the cost of spreading trading across screens. Theory sees a benefit as well.

In Chen and Duffie's model (2021), one asset trades on several exchanges at once. A trade moves the price only on the exchange where it happens, so traders can trade more aggressively — and, taken together, the exchanges' prices can end up more informative than a single exchange's would be.

So more venues bring a cost and a benefit. Which one wins is an empirical question: the one the paper asks of EU-Bonds.

The cost: dispersed flow

Each venue sees only a slice of the orders, so its screen lags behind the news and disagrees with the others.

The benefit: isolated impact

A trade moves only its own venue's price, so traders trade more boldly; and venues compete for the same orders, which can narrow spreads.
