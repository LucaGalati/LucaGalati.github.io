Is 4.3 points a lot?

It depends on where the score started — and the abstract reports the difference, not the levels.

Points are not percent. Suppose the controls stood still, so EU-Bonds' own score rose by 4.3 points. From 20% to 24.3% that is a rise of about a fifth; from 60% to 64.3%, a rise of about a fourteenth.

And a single estimate comes with a margin of error, which the abstract does not report.
