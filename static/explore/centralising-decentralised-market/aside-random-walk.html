Side path The variance ratio

Why a random walk scores 1.

An hour holds twelve five-minute steps. If each step ignores the one before it, their variances simply add up: the variance of the hour's move is twelve times the variance of one step, and the ratio of the two is 1.

Make each step undo part of the last, and the hour's moves bunch together: the ratio falls below 1. Make each step repeat part of the last, and they fan out: the ratio rises above 1.
