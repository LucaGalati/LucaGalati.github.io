Does Centralising a Decentralised Market Do Any Good?

Luca Galati and Loriana Pelizzon

A bond can trade on many exchanges at once, each showing its own price. In late 2023 the European Commission obliged the dealers in EU-Bonds to keep quotes alive on designated platforms. Did concentrating the quotes make prices any better? This explorable walks through what the paper measured before and after.
