An explorable explanation

The Effects of War on Market Functioning

Jonathan Federle, Luca Galati and Ryan Riordan · working paper

On 24 February 2022 a war began a few hundred kilometres from the stock exchanges of Warsaw, Budapest and Bucharest. This short explorable follows what it did to the cost of trading, on the first morning and over the twenty trading days that followed, and asks how much that depended on distance to the fighting. Two decks on the main path, three side paths, a handful of things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
