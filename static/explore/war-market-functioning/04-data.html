From 48,403 firms to 2,747

Every firm starts as a postal code. Headquarters are placed on the map through the GeoNames database, as in an earlier study by Federle and coauthors, and firms whose address cannot be located drop out first. Russia, Ukraine and the aerospace and defence sector are removed on purpose: the question is the spillover to third countries, not the belligerents or the arms makers.

LSEG Tick History then supplies every trade and every best quote over forty trading days, 27 January to 23 March 2022; the download held about 11.2 billion messages, 9.3 billion of them quote updates and 1.8 billion trades. Requiring a complete daily record for all forty days leaves 2,747 firms on 54 exchanges in 46 countries. That requirement keeps the firms that traded every day, the more liquid ones.

Six standard deviations from normal

Deck 1 measured the jolt in standard deviations, not in percent. Here is why, with an invented firm.

Each firm is compared with itself: the same trading hour on five ordinary days a fortnight before the invasion, days −10 to −6. Subtract its average there, divide by its usual wobble, its standard deviation, and you have the jolt.

A calm firm and a jumpy firm can show the same rise in percent and very different jolts. Measured this way, the jolt means the same thing for a firm in Warsaw and a firm in New York, so it can be averaged across them.

One equation, five ways to be exposed

Outcomefirm, day = β × Exposurefirm × Warday + controls + firm effect + day effect + noise

Warday switches on from 24 February. The firm effect absorbs everything permanently true of a firm: a Warsaw small-cap is always less liquid than a Frankfurt blue chip. The day effect absorbs everything true of a day for everyone: 24 February was a bad day everywhere. What is left, β, is how much more the exposed firms moved after the invasion than the rest.

  • Exposurebordering Ukraine; one border away; either; 1 ÷ (1 + distance in km); the firm’s own return on 24 February, with intraday volatility as a further check.
  • Controlslog market value, one over the share price, log number of quote updates.
  • Uncertaintystandard errors clustered by firm and by date.

Two checks

Parallel trends. Before the invasion, were exposed firms already drifting away from the rest? For firms bordering Ukraine, quoted spreads and trading show no differential trend; effective spreads do. The second ring’s trading and the distance measure for quoted spreads also show some drift, and the paper reports them all (Table 12).

A matched sample. Each of the 448 firms in the rings is paired with a distant firm of similar size and share price. That gives a similar picture: quoted-spread and trading effects survive; effective-spread effects shrink by about half (Appendix C).
