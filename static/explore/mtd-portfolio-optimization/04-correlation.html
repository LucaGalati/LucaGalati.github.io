A lead that correlation cannot see

Two invented stocks over 41 days; Follower tends to copy what Leader did the day before. On the same day the correlation is about zero. Leader today against Follower tomorrow, the pattern leaps out; turned around, it vanishes. The dependence has a direction, and a correlation network, one line per pair and no arrow, would miss it.

A correlation taken a day apart would catch this pair, but not the second one: after Leader moves, Follower goes flat; after a flat day, Follower moves, up or down. Even a day apart the correlation is about zero, yet the grid is far from even. The paper calls such pairs “dependent yet uncorrelated”. The MTD model works from counts like these grids, so it can see both.
