A Network-Based Mixture Transition Distribution Approach to Portfolio Optimization

Riccardo De Blasis, Luca Galati and Filippo Petroni · Quantitative Finance, 2026 (published online 23 June 2026)

Correlation tells you which stocks move together, never which one moves first. This explorable follows a paper that maps who leads whom among the stocks of three big indices, turns the map into a rule for choosing a portfolio, and tests the rule over twenty years. Three decks on the main path, four side paths, two bets and several things to play with: about twenty-five minutes.
