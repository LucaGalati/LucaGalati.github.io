Not Dead Yet: Options Trading Floors

Luca Galati, Terrence Hendershott, Saad Ali Khan and Ryan Riordan · working paper, draft of October 2026

In 2020 the Cboe options floor in Chicago closed for three months while its screens kept trading. This explorable follows what the paper learned from that switch: what the floor costs, and what it does that screens cannot.
