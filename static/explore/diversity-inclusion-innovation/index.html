Diversity, Inclusion, and Firm Performance: The Corporate Innovation Channel

Eleonora Monaco, Luca Galati, Lorenzo Dal Maso and Marco Maria Mattei · Business Strategy and the Environment, 2026

Many large firms are scaling back their diversity and inclusion programmes, and the research has never agreed on whether D&I pays. This paper follows 6,948 firms in 55 countries from 2010 to 2021 and asks a sharper question: if it pays, by which road? Here you build a D&I score, bet on what it goes with, and follow the detour through innovation. Three short decks on the main path, two side paths, about fifteen minutes.
