To fix or not to fix

The representativeness of the WM/R methodology that underpins the FX benchmark rates. A pre-registered report.

Matteo Benenchia, Luca Galati and Andrew Lepone · Pacific-Basin Finance Journal 84, 102311 · 2024

The currency market never closes, yet every afternoon at four o’clock in London it gets a closing price: the WM/R fix, computed from five minutes of trading and used to value portfolios around the world. This report is a plan written before its study was run. It asks whether that number still represents the market, above all on the last day of the month, and whether a ten- or twenty-minute window would do better. Build a fix from a stream of trades, bet on how much trading crowds into five minutes, and see exactly what the registered tests will measure: two decks on the main path, two side paths, about fifteen minutes.
