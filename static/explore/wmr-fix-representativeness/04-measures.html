Measuring the market on one trade

Every five-second bucket around 4 pm gets the same tape measure. Here it is on a single trade, so you can watch what each number does.

The letter q is the trade’s direction: +1 if a buyer started it, −1 if a seller did. That sign makes a sell that drags the price down count the same way as a buy that lifts it.

Price impact looks 20 seconds ahead. The authors read it as the cost a trader pays, minus the part that ends up as revenue for whoever supplied the liquidity.
