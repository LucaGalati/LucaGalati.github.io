The mayor’s companies

Every Italian town elects a mayor and a council for five years; a mayor can serve two terms in a row. Resignations, or a council dissolved for mafia infiltration, can force an early vote, so towns do not all vote in the same year. Each year one Sunday between 15 April and 15 June is set for every town that votes, well in advance.

The town is usually not only the majority owner of its utility company but also its main customer, paying for its services under contract. When it owns more than half, the mayor can appoint or remove the company’s director with very wide discretion. The mayor does not prepare the accounts, but holds both levers.
