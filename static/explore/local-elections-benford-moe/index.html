Local Elections and the Quality of Financial Statements in Municipally Owned Entities

Francesco Capalbo, Luca Galati, Claudio Lupi and Margherita Smarra · Chaos, Solitons and Fractals 173, 2023

Italian towns own the companies that run their buses, water, gas and rubbish collection, and those companies publish their accounts each spring, sometimes a few weeks before the town votes. This paper asks whether the accounts published just before a local election look different from the rest, without opening a single ledger: it counts first digits. You will bet on a strange law of numbers, operate the paper’s own tests, and see where the anomalies sit and what they can and cannot prove. Two decks on the main path, two side paths, about fifteen minutes.
