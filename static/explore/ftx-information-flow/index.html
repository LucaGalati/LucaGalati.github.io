An explorable explanation

Information Flow in the FTX Bankruptcy: A Network Approach

Riccardo De Blasis, Luca Galati, Rosanna Grassi and Giorgio Rizzini · Physica A, 2024

In November 2022 the crypto exchange FTX went from a news report to bankruptcy in nine days. This explorable turns a month of minute-by-minute prices into a map of which coins moved together, and asks which coins sat at the centre of that map before and during the collapse. You will cut links in the paper’s own small network, bet on which coin took the centre, and follow FTX’s own token, FTT, to the edge. Three decks on the main path, two side paths, about fifteen minutes.
