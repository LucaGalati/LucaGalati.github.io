Financial Contagion in Cryptocurrency Exchanges: Evidence from the FTT Collapse

Luca Galati, Alexander Webb and Robert I. Webb · Finance Research Letters, 2024

In November 2022 the crypto exchange FTX collapsed, and with it FTT, the coin it had issued itself. This short explorable follows the letter’s two questions: did the collapse spread to the other exchanges where FTT traded, and during the days when FTX still let customers trade but withdraw almost nothing, where did their money go? Two decks on the main path, two side paths, a few things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
