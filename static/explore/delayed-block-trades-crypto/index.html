An explorable explanation

The Information Content of Delayed Block Trades in Cryptocurrency Markets

Luca Galati and Riccardo De Blasis · The British Accounting Review, 2026

On 6 December 2019 the Gemini exchange halved the size a bitcoin trade needed before it could be done away from the order book and shown to everyone else ten minutes later. This explorable uses that one change to ask what a large trade tells the market, and whether the right to keep it quiet for ten minutes draws in the traders who know something. Three decks on the main path, three side paths, a few things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
