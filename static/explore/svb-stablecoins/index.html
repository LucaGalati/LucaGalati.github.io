An explorable explanation

Silicon Valley Bank Bankruptcy and Stablecoins Stability

Luca Galati and Francesco Capalbo · International Review of Financial Analysis 91 (2024), 103001

On 11 March 2023, the day after Silicon Valley Bank failed, the company behind USD Coin said that 8% of the coin’s reserves were stuck in the failed bank. A coin built to be worth exactly one dollar traded below 87 cents. This explorable follows that weekend: what a stuck reserve does to a stablecoin, where frightened money ran, and how the authors test whether a bank’s failure spread into crypto markets. Two decks on the main path, two side paths, a few things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
