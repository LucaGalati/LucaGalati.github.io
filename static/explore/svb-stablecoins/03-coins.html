Five dollars and a Bitcoin

A stablecoin is a crypto token built to hold a steady one-to-one peg to the US dollar, so traders can park money in crypto in a form worth a dollar, even on exchanges such as Binance that do not trade against dollars at all. Because they move between exchanges quickly, stablecoins sometimes trade a little above a dollar.

  • USDCUSD Coin, run by Circle: in March 2023 the second-largest stablecoin and the fifth-largest cryptocurrency by market value, with about $40 billion behind it, $3.3 billion of that at SVB.
  • USDTTether: the third-largest cryptocurrency by market value, and the most traded.
  • BUSDThe Binance dollar, the exchange’s own stablecoin. Binance does not trade it against dollars, so the paper converts its price against Tether into dollars.
  • DAIA stablecoin the paper counts among the less authoritative ones.
  • TUSDTrueUSD, which completes the study’s five most liquid stablecoins.
  • BTCBitcoin, the most liquid cryptocurrency, with no peg at all.
