A co-movement meter with a memory

The BEKK model keeps, for every pair of coins, a running estimate of how they move together, and updates it every minute. Each new estimate blends three things:

estimate now = (1 − a − b) × long-run level + a × (last move of A × last move of B) + b × estimate a minute ago

The weight a sets how much the latest minute counts; the weight b sets how much of the old estimate survives, the meter’s memory. The paper keeps both positive with a + b < 1, so the meter always drifts back to its long-run level.
