An explorable explanation

Reporting Delays and the Information Content of Off-Market Trades

Alex Frino, Luca Galati and Dionigi Gerace · Journal of Futures Markets, 2022

Large futures trades are often agreed in private and reported to the exchange afterwards. Until February 2018, the parties to any such block in FTSE 100 index futures on ICE Futures Europe could ask for it to be published up to an hour late, and for that hour the rest of the market was in the dark. This explorable follows what happened when the hour was taken away from the smaller of these trades, and asks whether hiding a trade for a while can make prices better informed. Two decks on the main path, three side paths, one bet and a few things to play with, about fifteen minutes.
