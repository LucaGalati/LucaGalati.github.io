An explorable explanation

Proportional Appropriation Systems
and Financial Statement Quality
in Municipally Owned Entities

Francesco Capalbo, Luca Galati, Claudio Lupi and Margherita SmarraJournal of Public Budgeting, Accounting & Financial Management, 2024

Italian cities own the companies that run their buses, collect their waste and pipe their water. From the 2015 accounts a new rule tied the two together: when such a company reports a loss, the city must set aside part of its own budget in proportion to its stake. Here you can work the rule yourself, then follow the paper into the first digits of the companies’ reported results, to see whether, and where, the books began to bend. Three decks on the main path, three side paths, about fifteen minutes.
