Side path · The sample

From 262,315 entities to 2,120

Italy’s Finance Ministry publishes an open database of every entity in which a public body holds shares. The authors keep those owned by a municipality, match them to company accounts in the AIDA database, and then keep only companies present in both five-year periods with a net income for all ten years, so the same companies are compared before and under the rule.

Where several cities own the same company, the stake that counts is that of the largest one, which holds the most formal power. That stake sorts each company into a group.
