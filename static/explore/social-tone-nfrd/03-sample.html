From 750 reports to 329

The authors collected 750 sustainability reports by hand, from company websites and the Corporate Register database, covering 2002 to 2022. Financial data and social scores come from Refinitiv Eikon, governance data and industry codes from NRG Metrics.

Three cuts follow. Firms with fewer than 500 employees go, because the directive does not cover them; then the years outside the ten-year window; then, the largest cut, reports without a social score or financial data. What remains is 329 reports from 83 firms, about four per firm.

The longest reports are not the most social

The average report, industry by industry. Energy and health-care firms give the largest share of their words to social matters. Utilities give the smallest, although their reports are by far the longest, about 117,000 words on average; the paper reads that as volume over social content.

Sort by either column and watch the order change. Because social tone is a share, a long report does not score higher by being long.
