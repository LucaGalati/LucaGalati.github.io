Beyond NFRD Compliance:
Is Social Tone Associated with Better Corporate Social Performance?

Eleonora Monaco, Luca Galati and Matteo Merlo · Financial Reporting, 2026

Since 2017, large listed firms in the European Union have had to report on how they treat their workers, customers and communities. This explorable counts the words in those reports the way the paper does, and asks whether writing more about social matters went with better social ratings, before the rule and after it. Two short decks on the main path, three side paths, a word counter and a bet: about fifteen minutes.
